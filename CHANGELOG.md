# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Opaque chain-neutral network/native/token identity and exact positive amounts.
- Versioned transaction/event and venue/account-scoped references.
- Explicit stable caller timestamps and source-scoped replay metadata.
- Balanced transfer, deposit, withdrawal, trade, and fee-only event drafts.
- Separate native gas and generic service fees, with gross fee semantics.
- Account-role, asset/precision, network, alias, and definition consistency checks.
- Integration with finance-ledger's validation/posting/reversal lifecycle.
- March 0.5.0 pin, generated properties, and compile-time contract fixtures.

Local path dependencies require sibling finance-core and finance-ledger projects.
No remote publishing, chain connection, signing, or serialization is included.
